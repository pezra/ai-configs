# Tickets and bug reports

## 9. Genre playbook (excerpt)

**Stories / tickets.** Title is a flat declarative of the outcome, not a category label. First line states what will be true when this is done. Acceptance criteria as 3–6 short parallel fragments, no nesting. Name known limits in their own line ("This does not cover X."). No first person.

**Bug reports.** Open with the broken behavior in one present-tense sentence — not the investigation, not the suspicion. Reproduction as 3–6 numbered steps, then expected, then actual, nothing between them. Evidence gets one colon lead-in, no narration after. Separate what you know from what you suspect, in that order, and label the suspicion: "I have not confirmed the cause." Severity flat and unhedged.
