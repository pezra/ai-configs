# Resumes

A resume names what Peter built and with what, and lets the reader infer the rest. Derived from Peter's resume (last revised 2014) and its per-application supplements. That's one document, so follow its structure and not its phrasing.

## Write it like this

- Capital "I" here and in cover letters; this is the formal external document the lowercase rule exempts. First person appears only in the summary, and rarely there.
- The summary opens with the subject dropped, naming role and specialty: "Full stack software engineer with special expertise in APIs and web services." At most two more sentences.
- Each role: title, linked company, then "Month YYYY - Month YYYY" ("present" for the current one). Keep real titles as they were, even odd ones ("API Badass").
- Bullets are verb-led fragments with no terminal period: "Led development of new open PaaS cloud solution from inception to release". Past tense for past roles, present tense for the current one. Don't mix in noun-phrase bullets ("Design and implementation of...").
- Name the system, tech and domain instead of adjectives: "Cassandra data model design for high volume IOT telemetry data", not "designed a sophisticated, scalable data layer".
- Skills and keywords go in a flat list. The heading can carry the one dry touch ("Buzzwords"); nothing else does.
- Tailor by choosing which roles and bullets to include, or swapping in a supplement for older work. Don't inflate a bullet to fit a job posting.

## Don't invent

Resumes are where an invented detail does the most harm. Never add a metric, team size, user count, revenue figure, outcome or technology Peter didn't give. Write `Reduced p99 latency [Peter: by how much?]` rather than guessing a number, and leave the bullet without a number if Peter has none.

## Leave out

- Résumé clichés, including ones in Peter's old resume: "passionate", "sophisticated", "effective", "highly maintainable and scalable", "results-driven", "team player"
- Objective statements, "References available upon request", and anything describing Peter's personality instead of work

## Extra checks

1. Every number, scope and outcome traces to something Peter provided.
2. Every bullet starts with a verb in the right tense and has no period.
3. No adjective is doing the work a named system or technology should do.
